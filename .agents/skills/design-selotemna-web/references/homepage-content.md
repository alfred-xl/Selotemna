# Selotemna Homepage Content Direction

## Confirmed company direction

Selotemna has two primary public divisions:

1. Real Estate Development
2. Engineering & Construction

Land and property opportunities sit under Real Estate Development. Projects are a core website destination. Property Management is not a primary homepage division and may be referenced only as a possible supporting service until its future placement is confirmed.

Primary conversion: Book or request an inspection.

## Homepage content principle

The implemented homepage is a concise corporate overview, not the destination for every detail. It introduces the company, presents the two divisions, features a small amount of opportunity and project content, and routes visitors to dedicated pages.

Detailed company history, division explanations, project catalogues, project details, inspection requirements, FAQs, and contact information belong on their respective pages.

## Implemented navigation

- Home
- About
- Real Estate Development
- Engineering & Construction
- Projects
- FAQ
- Contact

Book Inspection remains a prominent action rather than a general navigation label where space is limited.

## Implemented hero

The hero identifies Selotemna and expresses the relationship between Real Estate Development and Engineering & Construction.

Eyebrow: `Real Estate Development · Engineering & Construction`

H1: `Developing real estate. Delivering engineered solutions.`

Primary CTA: Book an Inspection

Secondary CTA: Explore Projects

## Division pathways

### Real Estate Development

Introduce Selotemna’s land, property, and development opportunities. Route detailed inventory and opportunity content to the Real Estate Development page.

### Engineering & Construction

Introduce Selotemna’s engineering and construction capability. Route detailed scope, enquiries, and verified work to the Engineering & Construction page.

### Property Management

Do not present Property Management as a third primary division. It may appear later as a supporting service after its scope and placement are confirmed.

## Projects overview

The homepage includes a concise Projects preview linking to the Projects page. The project architecture supports:

- Ongoing Projects
- Completed Projects
- Upcoming Projects

Only show a category when appropriate content exists. Do not assign a status without verification.

Temporary project content may be used for development and layout testing only. It is identified as layout content outside production, automatically removed in production, and never presented as verified Selotemna work.

## Omu Creek

Omu Creek remains the verified featured land opportunity under Real Estate Development.

Preserve these facts exactly:

- Land allocation
- Lagos State Government Allocation
- ₦50,000 per sqm
- 300 sqm: ₦15,000,000
- 500 sqm: ₦25,000,000
- 1,000 sqm: ₦50,000,000
- Prices exclude applicable taxes.
- Availability and property information are subject to confirmation.

Do not classify Omu Creek as ongoing, completed, or upcoming until its status is confirmed.

The approved FAQ source verifies additional Omu Creek information. Use references/faq-content.md for its current location, title claims, payment terms, charges, documentation, infrastructure, allocation, construction, default, resale, and refund wording. Do not infer beyond that source.

Primary CTA: Request an Omu Creek Inspection

## About summary

The homepage may contain a short About summary linking to the About page. Do not put the complete company story on the homepage or invent history, operating locations, statistics, or differentiators.

## Inspection summary

The homepage may briefly explain that visitors choose an opportunity, share their preferred date and contact information, and receive follow-up from a Selotemna representative.

Submitting a request does not automatically confirm an inspection appointment. The detailed flow belongs on the Book Inspection page.

## FAQ

The approved 15-item Omu Creek FAQ source is recorded in references/faq-content.md.

Keep the homepage concise with these four questions:

1. What is Omu Creek Estate?
2. What is the title on the land?
3. What plot sizes are available for purchase?
4. How much is a plot of land at Omu Creek Estate?

The dedicated FAQ page contains the complete 15-item set. Continue stating elsewhere that an inspection submission is a request and does not automatically confirm an appointment.

## Final conversion

Keep the final homepage conversion concise and route visitors to Book Inspection, verified contact channels, or the relevant division/page.

Show WhatsApp and telephone only when verified.

## SEO direction

Page titles and descriptions use the two-division terminology and verified opportunity content.

Do not add project status, location, structured data, or proof that has not been verified.
