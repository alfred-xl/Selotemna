# Selotemna FAQ Content

## Status

## General Information and Location

### 1. What is Omu Creek Estate?

Omu Creek Estate is a planned residential community intended for people looking to build homes or invest in land.

### 2. Who is marketing Omu Creek Estate?

The estate is exclusively marketed and managed by Selotemna Limited.

### 3. Where is Omu Creek Estate located?

Omu Creek Estate covers Eti-Osa LGA, Ibeju-Lekki LGA, and Epe LGA.

## Land Title, Sizes, and Pricing

### 4. What is the title on the land?

The land is free from government acquisition and adverse claims. It is covered by a Lagos State Government Allocation.

### 5. What plot sizes are available for purchase?

Available options are:

- Commercial plot: 1,000 square metres
- Standard plot: 500 square metres
- Half plot: 300 square metres

### 6. How much is a plot of land at Omu Creek Estate?

Current outright prices are:

- 1,000 sqm: ₦50,000,000
- 500 sqm: ₦25,000,000
- 300 sqm: ₦15,000,000

Prices exclude applicable taxes. Availability and property information are subject to confirmation.

## Payments and Additional Charges

### 7. Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Selotemna Limited offers a payment plan with an initial deposit of ₦5,000,000 and the balance spread over six months. Installment plans may attract a premium compared with outright payment.

### 8. Are there any other charges besides the cost of the land?

The following statutory fees apply for legal documentation and physical estate development:

- Deed of Assignment: 5%
- Registered Survey: ₦1,500,000
- Development Levy: ₦5,000,000 per 500 sqm

### 9. What documents do I receive after payment?

- After the initial deposit: Payment Receipt and Contract of Sale
- After full payment for the land: Payment Receipt and Letter of Acknowledgment
- After payment of statutory fees: Deed of Assignment, Survey Plan, and physical Allocation Letter

## Infrastructure and Allocation

### 10. What infrastructure will Selotemna Limited provide?

Planned infrastructure and amenities include:

- Perimeter fencing and a secure gatehouse
- Graded road network
- Drainage system
- Electricity infrastructure
- Recreational green areas

### 11. When do I get physical allocation of my plot?

Physical allocation is conducted within 30 days after complete payment for the land and confirmation of the survey fee, upon completion of the Omu Creek Bridge.

### 12. Can I start building immediately after allocation?

After physical allocation and collection of the building-layout guidelines from the estate management, buyers may commence fencing and construction.

## Legal and Administrative Policies

### 13. What happens if I default on my installment payments?

Buyers are encouraged to communicate with Selotemna if they experience unexpected financial delays. Prolonged default without notice may lead to revocation of the contract or reallocation to another plot, subject to a 20% administrative charge under the default policy.

### 14. Can I resell my land later?

Yes. Omu Creek land may be resold to a third party. Selotemna Limited must be informed for security and documentation purposes, and a standard change-of-ownership fee of 10% applies.

### 15. Is there a refund policy?

Yes. If a buyer discontinues the transaction before full payment, a refund can be processed. A 30% administrative and agency fee will be deducted, and the refund is typically paid within 60 days of the request.
